I wanted a space that values **clarity over presentation**.

Most personal sites are portfolios. Mine is meant to be something else — a record of what I build, what I learn, and how my thinking evolves.

## The Problem With Portfolio Sites

Portfolio sites are optimised for impression. Everything is polished. Every project listed is finished. Every description is written for an employer.

That's not useful to me.

I'm at the beginning of my engineering path. Most of what I do is unfinished, uncertain, and in progress. I want a site that reflects that honestly — not one that pretends I already know where I'm going.

## What I Actually Want

A place to:

- Document what I'm learning *as I learn it*
- Write about what fails, not just what works
- Store my thinking so I can refer to it later
- Track how my understanding of a topic changes over time

## So That's What This Is

This site is not a portfolio. It's a log.

Every post here is a snapshot of where I was when I wrote it. Some of it will age badly. That's fine. The goal isn't to be impressive — it's to be *accurate*.

> Good engineering is not about writing more code, but about writing the right code.

I'm not sure I can always write the right code yet. But I can write honestly about what I'm trying to do.

That's enough for now.
